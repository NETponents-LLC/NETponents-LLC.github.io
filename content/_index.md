## About NETponents

NETponents LLC is a boutique engineering firm in Saline, Michigan. Every project is led and built by a senior backend and DevOps engineer who also designs digital hardware by hand: schematics, PCB layout, firmware, and bring-up.

That mix is uncommon. Most teams are strong on one side of the wall: the electronics, or the cloud. We work on both, so the data your equipment produces arrives somewhere secure, structured, and useful, without a handoff between vendors.

**Where we focus:** custom digital electronics, microcontroller and MPU carrier boards, IoT prototypes, wired and local telemetry, and the Go and Python services that ingest and present the data.

**What we don't do:** high-end audio, or custom RF and FCC-regulated wireless devices. Knowing our limits keeps projects on schedule.
