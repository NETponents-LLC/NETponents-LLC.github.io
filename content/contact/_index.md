We don't sell off-the-shelf products or subscriptions. We solve specific problems for businesses with physical equipment, data-logging needs, or custom digital requirements.

## How we work

1. **You reach out with an operational problem.** Describe what you're trying to measure, control, or automate, and what it costs you today. A rough idea is fine.
2. **We run a structured discovery and feasibility phase.** We define requirements, evaluate approaches, identify risks, and give you a clear scope, timeline, and estimate before you commit to a build.
3. **We deliver a custom engineering solution.** Hardware, firmware, cloud pipeline, and documentation, built and tested as one system, with the design files and source you need to own the result.
